## Explicit recovered source construction

The shared background in every twin pair is generated as a broad positive single-hotspot, double-hotspot, or flare/drift analytic scene and projected by a fixed weighted least-squares map into the frozen Movie007 595-dimensional source class. Its coefficients remain unknown to the inverse. The projection map is built once from the 15 x 48 x 25 registered latent grid and the Movie007 basis; no test outcome enters it.

Each old alternative is a positive analytic feature multiplied by the Movie010 compact C2 window: zero for tau >= -6M and tau <= -31M, one on [-29,-10]M, with quintic transitions. The four pair constructions are fixed before sampling:

1. narrow hotspot: equal radial/temporal parameters with opposite azimuthal offsets about a common orbit;
2. shearing spiral: identical envelope with equal-and-opposite radial phase shear;
3. split/merge: the same two-lobe movie with orthogonal splitting axes;
4. radial plume: identical envelope with opposite radial drift directions.

Parameter ranges are those already carried by Movie009–011: feature amplitude 0.35–0.55, radial centre 7.3–11.7M, radial width 0.30–0.60M, angular concentration 9–15, angular speed 0.05–0.16, shear magnitude 0.35–0.80, split angle 0.50–1.10, radial drift 0.25–0.70M, and plume bend 0.15–0.50. Sources are admitted only for finite values, nonnegative emissivity, and activity on at least nine old frames; order-1 response and reconstruction performance are never used.

## Explicit observation preprocessing

For each arm, fit the registered rank-256 randomized observation PCA to the complete training set's **clean q8 base-subtracted observations represented at both SNR0=100 and 300 after noise whitening**. Per-row mean and scale are computed from that same duplicated clean training population. Training noise is still redrawn every epoch, and the projected noise covariance induced by row scaling and the orthonormal observation PCA is sampled exactly in score space. This avoids generating full 832/1664-dimensional noise solely to discard it after the registered projection.

The source PCA is fit to all 1,080 training sibling movies on the registered 15 x 48 x 25 grid, using randomized rank 192 and seed 120090. The physical q8/q12 latent response matrices are computed by a sparse periodic trilinear grid-to-ray integral; clean validation and test observations remain direct analytic q12 evaluations, so the latent representation and interpolation error remain testable.
