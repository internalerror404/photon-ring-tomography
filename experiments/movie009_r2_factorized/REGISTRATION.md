## 1. Scientific hypothesis

Movie009 established that the first indirect image distinguishes natural old histories that are exactly absent from the direct acquisition, but the global rich ridge estimator did not satisfy the 95%-reliable frame-by-frame movie endpoint. Movie010 and Movie011 showed that neither controlling the background representation nor exact linear-basis nesting was sufficient. Movie013 showed that invertible row-coordinate changes, a larger nested linear source class, and separate quadratic old/background penalties do not solve the problem.

Movie009-R2 tests a more specific missing ingredient:

> Estimate the shared/current background from direct light first; subtract its predicted first-indirect contribution; reconstruct only the compact old innovation with a dynamics-aware latent prior; then jointly refine background and innovation under the frozen Kerr data model.

The hypothesis is that one global linear source basis and one origin-centred quadratic penalty force well-observed current structure and weak high-bandwidth old structure to compete for the same coefficients. Conditional factorization should reduce that interference without creating new photons.

## 3. Fresh source populations

Movie009-R2 uses fresh populations under explicit formulas; it does not claim byte-for-byte reproduction of the unavailable original Movie009 source implementation.

### Shared/current background

Every twin pair shares one unknown background lying exactly in the frozen Movie007 595-dimensional source class. Backgrounds are generated by projecting broad single-hotspot, double-hotspot, and flare/drift scenes into that class, then applying a positivity scale if required. The inverse receives no background coefficients.

### Natural old alternatives

Each sibling equals `1 + background + old_feature_A/B`. The old alternatives are evaluated analytically at q8/q12 ray landing coordinates and on the movie grid; they are never projected before observation or scoring.

A compact C2 window is zero for `tau>=-6M` and `tau<=-31M`, one on `[-29,-10]M`, with quintic transitions. Direct twin means must therefore agree to numerical roundoff by support disjointness.

Four families:

1. narrow orbiting hotspot: two phase-separated high-concentration spots;
2. shearing spiral/arc: equal envelopes with opposite radial phase shear;
3. split/merge scene: equal two-lobe envelopes with orthogonal time-dependent axes;
4. radial plume: equal envelopes with opposite radial drift; excluded from training and validation.

No candidate is admitted using order-1 response or reconstruction performance. Admission uses finite values, nonnegative total emissivity, activity on at least nine old frames, and fixed minimum support-weighted feature RMS.

## 4. Methods

### M0 — global Movie007 ridge baseline

Use the frozen 595-dimensional source class and original arm-specific Movie007 ridge exponents (`+1` direct, `-2` labelled). This is a control, not expected to represent high-bandwidth old features.

### M1 — background-conditioned latent ridge

1. Estimate background coefficients from direct q8 data using the old 595-dimensional operator. Select one background exponent from `-6,...,3` using validation total-movie error at SNR300.
2. Predict and subtract that background's q8 order-1 contribution.
3. Learn a rank-96 PCA decoder from training old-feature movies only, on a fixed 15 radial x 48 periodic azimuthal x 25 temporal grid over `[6,13]M x [-pi,pi) x [-32,28]M`.
4. Build q8/q12 latent response matrices by periodic trilinear interpolation of the frozen decoder at ray coordinates, followed by the existing pixel integration.
5. Map the whitened order-1 residual to latent scores with ridge regression; choose one exponent from `-8,...,2` using validation support-movie error only.
6. Jointly refine background and latent innovation by minimizing the direct and order-1 q8 residuals plus quadratic trust penalties centred on the two stagewise estimates. Select one trust exponent pair from a fixed 5x5 grid on validation only.

### M2 — background-conditioned latent MLP

Use the same background estimate, source PCA decoder, q8/q12 latent response matrices, and final joint refinement as M1. Replace the residual-to-latent ridge map by a two-hidden-layer MLP:

- input: rank-128 PCA scores of standardized whitened order-1 residuals;
- hidden widths: 256 and 256 with SiLU;
- output: 96 source-latent scores;
- seeds `11,22,33`;
- AdamW, 100 epochs, batch size 64, learning rate `3e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`;
- training noise redrawn each epoch with SNR sampled uniformly from `{100,300}`;
- arithmetic mean of the three latent predictions is primary;
- no architecture, width, epoch, or loss sweep.

The MLP learns only the inverse map. The Kerr renderer, source decoder, noise law, frame metric, and joint-refinement objective remain fixed.

## 5. Endpoints

For every method and arm report:

- individual twin identification and pair-both-correct rate, with balanced direct ties;
- total-movie and differential-movie frame error/correlation;
- 95% and 90% reliable contiguous spans under the unchanged Movie007 rule (`error<=0.35`, `correlation>=0.75`, inactive predicted RMS `<=0.05`);
- paired old-frame error reduction by family;
- background-only error, innovation-only error, and total error separately;
- q8/q12 clean and fitted detector discrepancies;
- source-PCA representation floor and held-out radial-plume floor;
- bootstrap intervals clustered by twin pair;
- wall time and peak memory.

The original Movie009 summary is quoted only as prior evidence. Movie009-R2 is judged on its fresh confirmatory population.

## 6. Primary success gate

At SNR300, M2 neural-ensemble labelled results must satisfy all conditions:

1. input authentication and Movie007 replay pass;
2. direct clean twin difference `<=1e-10` whitened at q8 and q12 for every test pair;
3. all sources remain nonnegative and every family fills without response-based admission;
4. direct identification lies in `[0.45,0.55]` and direct pair-both is at most 5%;
5. labelled individual identification `>=0.95` and pair-both `>=0.90`;
6. labelled 95%-reliable total-movie span `>=12M` and at least `8M` beyond direct;
7. at least three of four families, including held-out radial plume, have positive median paired total-movie error reduction;
8. at least 90% of labelled differential movies pass every active frame, while at most 5% of direct differential movies do;
9. q8/q12 clean and fitted discrepancies are `<=5e-4` relatively and `<=0.1` whitened.

A valid M1 pass with M2 failure is reported as a factorization/latent-linear result, not a neural result. Failure preserves the source bank, latent decoder, checkpoints, and all negative endpoints; no post-outcome change to source families, thresholds, latent rank, architecture, or gate is authorized.

## 7. Scope and limits

A pass would support background-conditioned recovery of natural direct-null historical movies on one Kerr chart with ideal order labels. It would not establish full-annulus, visibility-domain, telescope, or observational recovery, and prior-conditioned completion outside the measurement-supported subspace must be labelled separately.

Resource limits: zero new physical calls, zero Paper-I units, CPU float64 for linear algebra, neural float32 allowed, one BLAS thread for scientific NumPy/SciPy, <=8GiB memory, <=45 minutes total local execution.
