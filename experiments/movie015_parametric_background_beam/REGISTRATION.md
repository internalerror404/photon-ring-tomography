## 1. Scientific hypothesis

The shared background belongs to one of three nonlinear dynamical families: broad single hotspot, broad double hotspot, or flare/drift. Direct data can be compatible with distinct family/parameter basins whose first-indirect forecasts differ greatly. A beam drawn only around one linear MAP cannot represent that multimodality.

Movie015 tests:

> Search globally across the registered background families and parameter ranges, retain several direct-compatible basins, and infer the background and historical family jointly from direct plus order-1 data.

## 3. Background hypothesis library

Before validation/test evaluation, generate a deterministic library with seed `150090`:

- 1,024 backgrounds from each of the three registered families;
- parameters drawn from the exact Movie009-R2 background ranges;
- each analytic scene projected into the old 595-dimensional class exactly as in source generation;
- q8/q12 direct and order-1 responses and source-grid movies cached;
- no candidate admitted or rejected using validation/test response or reconstruction performance.

For each observed direct movie:

1. score all 3,072 background hypotheses under the full direct whitened likelihood plus the frozen source prior;
2. keep the top 16 hypotheses from each family and the best 32 globally after stable tie-breaking;
3. combine those 32 backgrounds with the frozen Movie014 4,096-element family-blind historical library using the same deterministic 32-per-family historical shortlist;
4. score all joint hypotheses under direct plus order-1 data;
5. retain the top 16 joint hypotheses and form both a MAP movie and posterior-weighted movie.

The true background family and parameters are never supplied. No local continuous optimization is allowed in Stage A.
