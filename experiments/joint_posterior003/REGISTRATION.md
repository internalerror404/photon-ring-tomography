## Exactly specified inference model

There are three unknown background-family indices k with prior probability 1/3 each. Generate 64 independent background parameter draws per family using the inherited analytic source functions. Project them into the new CACHE001 595-dimensional basis using its explicitly defined separable discrete source-volume least-squares rule. For each projected background use the coefficient bound m0 - sum_m hypot(cos_m,sin_m) at each radial/time coefficient to lower-bound the continuous contrast. When 1+lower < .15, rescale to lower >= -.82. This is a source-only positivity adjustment, never a detector-response or reconstruction admission decision. It is a new projection convention, not recovery of the missing Movie007 projection code.

For family k let mu_bk be its 64-source sample mean and F_bk its centered sample columns divided by sqrt(63). The model is b=mu_bk+F_bk z_b, z_b~N(0,I_64). These are continuous field coefficients and an inferred family index; the original phase/speed/radius parameters are not inferred by this approximation.

## Uncertainty semantics and calibration

Compute exact scalar mixture-normal CDFs at every true grid value. This reports marginal 90/95 coverage without confusing moment-matched normal intervals with actual mixture quantiles. Compute exact 95% interval widths for the five scalar functionals by bisection. Also report posterior SD and between-family variance contribution across the grid.

Raw whole-movie Bayesian bands are conservative Bonferroni rectangles over all 30720 registered grid points: marginal tail alpha/(2*30720). Under the declared Gaussian mixture these have posterior mass at least 1-alpha, not exactly 1-alpha. They are not continuous-source or frequentist coverage guarantees under analytic-family mismatch.

A separately labelled split-conformal band uses score=max |truth-mean|/max(posterior_SD,1e-12) over all grid points, both siblings and four draws in a calibration pair. Within each of three calibration families choose order statistic ceil((n+1)*level), n=24, for level .90/.95. The deployed factor is the maximum across the three family factors: no test family label chooses an interval. Freeze separate direct/joint factors before test-source generation. Conditional exchangeability supports conservative coverage for those three specified source populations; held-out plume has no such automatic guarantee and must be reported separately. Band widening never changes point reconstructions or repairs a movie-fidelity failure.

Report 2000-resample history-pair-cluster bootstrap intervals, stratified by the four fixed test families. For pair-level all-sibling/all-draw band coverage report a one-sided 95% Chernoff-KL lower bound on average coverage; this accommodates independent but non-identically distributed family strata instead of assuming one homogeneous binomial. Extra empirical uncertainty gates: observed coverage >=.90/.95 and lower bound >=.85/.90 respectively. These pilot gates allow a five-point undercoverage tolerance; they do not certify exact nominal coverage. Distinguish conservative raw posterior bands, calibrated bands, and OOD plume results.
