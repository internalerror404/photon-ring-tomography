# Mahakal II Movie010 — background-controlled off-basis direct-null movies

**Lineage:** successor to `MOVIE009_STAGEA_FAIL` at commit `3fe4c7d1d67d08eef4c0cfb2907651b024573d4c`. Movie009 correctly identified every natural analytic twin but failed its 95%-reliable total-movie span because the shared/current background was itself outside both reconstruction classes. Movie010 changes only that confound in a new experiment; it does not relabel or overwrite Movie009.

**Status at registration:** no Movie010 background, feature pair, observation, regularization decision, reconstruction, neural weight, or endpoint has been generated. No new Kerr ray, path integral, hull/critical root, or Paper-I unit is authorized.

## Question

Can the first indirect Kerr image reconstruct a natural historical movie difference that is analytically outside the original 595-dimensional class and exactly absent from direct imaging, when the shared current/background movie is itself representable and therefore does not independently fail the anchor frames?

## Frozen acquisition and metric

Reuse the authenticated Movie007 q8 inverse/q12 reference acquisition: Kerr `a=0.5`, inclination `50 deg`, static observer at `100M`, ideal order labels `n=0,1`, 8x8 pixels per order, 13 observer times `0,2,...,24M`, one direct-reference noise density shared across orders, and Movie007's 15-frame union measurement-support metric at `tau=0,-2,...,-28M`. Pixel-integrated rows and `sigma_Omega^2 dOmega` noise remain unchanged. Required binary hashes are the same as Movie009.

## Shared background control

Generate fresh broad Movie007 source histories from its registered single-hotspot, double-hotspot, and flare/drift families, project each into the frozen 595-dimensional source class, and use the projected field as the shared background truth. This is not a known background in the inverse: its coefficients remain unknown and are reconstructed from the data. It is only guaranteed to lie in the declared class, removing the unrelated current-background representation mismatch found in Movie009.

Use 8 validation and 16 held-out backgrounds per natural old-feature family. Background families are balanced cyclically. Validation seed `100061`, test seed `100062`; every candidate and projection/positivity check is retained.

## Natural historical twins

Use the four analytic old-feature families from Movie009—narrow orbiting hotspot, opposite-shear spiral/arc, split/merge alternatives, and opposite radial plumes—but generate fresh parameters with seeds `100071` and `100072`. The features are evaluated directly at q12 physical ray landing points and on the source movie grid; they are never projected before rendering or scoring.

The temporal envelope is a compact C2 plateau: zero outside `(-31,-6)M`, one on `[-29,-10]M`, with quintic smooth transitions. Thus each sibling difference is exactly zero on all direct-ray source times while remaining active through the registered oldest frame. Primary feature amplitudes are drawn from 0.35–0.55 and are not admitted using order-1 response or reconstruction performance. Sources are admitted only by finite arithmetic, nonnegative emissivity, and registered support-frame activity.

For each shared background `b` and alternatives `f_A,f_B`, the siblings are `j_A=1+b+f_A` and `j_B=1+b+f_B`. Direct and labelled arms share the same direct samples and direct-noise draw. Four matched draws at SNR0=300 are primary; SNR0=100 is secondary.

## Stage A estimators

1. Frozen Movie007 595-dimensional ridge, with its source norm, penalty and arm-specific exponents (`+1` direct, `-2` labelled).
2. The same 1617-dimensional rich class used in Movie009: 7 cubic radial splines, Fourier factors through m=5, and 21 linear temporal splines. Rebuild it from cached ray tuples. Select one ridge exponent per arm from `k=-7,...,3` using validation histories only and the median support-weighted movie error at SNR0=300. No test- or family-specific tuning.

## Stage A endpoints and gate

Report individual sibling identification, pair-both-correct, differential-feature movie fidelity, total-movie frame error/correlation, 95% and 90% reliable spans, per-family paired improvement, full-annulus control, and q8/q12 clean/fitted response discrepancies.

Stage A passes only if: inputs/replays and q8/q12 gates pass; direct identification is chance with pair-both at most 5%; rich labelled identification is at least 90% and pair-both at least 80%; rich labelled 95%-reliable total-movie span is at least 12M and exceeds direct by at least 8M; at least three of four families including radial plume improve; and no source is selected by response.

## Stage B authorization

A Stage-A pass authorizes a separately frozen Retarded Neural Field evaluation using the architecture already specified in Movie009, but with Movie010 source populations and no architecture/loss changes after Stage-A outcomes. A Stage-A fail blocks neural execution. Any neural result must keep the Kerr renderer fixed and report completion outside the measured/old finite subspaces separately.

## Scope

This is one Kerr chart with ideal order labels and synthetic source movies. A pass would establish background-controlled off-basis historical movie recovery in the tested source families. It would not establish full-annulus, visibility-domain, telescope, or observational black-hole movie recovery.
