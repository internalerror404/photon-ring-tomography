# Movie009-R5 preexecution amendment 001 — lazy q12 and movie evaluation

Before generating any bank candidate or reading any test outcome, evaluate every candidate on the q8 inverse rays used for family-blind selection. Evaluate q12 responses and movie-grid fields only for the selected candidates and the fixed runner-up diagnostics.

This is computationally equivalent for candidate selection because neither q12 data nor movie metrics enter the registered selection score. It avoids storing q12 responses and 30,720 movie values for all 8,192 candidates. The q8/q12 numerical gate remains mandatory on every selected fitted field and is unchanged.

Candidate counts, generation order, parameter distributions, amplitude bounds, test observations, selection rule, tie rule, metrics, and success thresholds do not change.
