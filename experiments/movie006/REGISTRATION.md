# Mahakal II movie006 — prospective movie-recovery pilot

**Base:** `b63bb905fc83cfc5b696c06e0338b8bea96e9e2c` on the separate Paper-II line.  
**Purpose:** test whether a physically generated first-indirect Kerr channel extends reliable reconstruction of a spatially structured source movie beyond what the direct channel can recover.  
**Status at registration:** geometry-only scouting completed; no movie source, observation, regularization selection, inverse result, or movie metric has been generated. Scouting established one rectangular chart on which both the first and second equatorial crossings are emitting and smooth.

This is a development pilot, not a sealed journal main. It may return a negative result. Paper I, its maps, and its remaining 854 convention-B units are not used or modified.

## 1. Fixed physical renderer

- Kerr parameters: `M=1`, spin `a=0.5`, inclination `i=50°`, static observer at Boyer–Lindquist radius `100M`.
- Equatorial, optically thin scalar source with prograde circular-emitter redshift law.
- Common chart coordinates: `lambda ∈ [0.5,3.0]`, `z=log(r_turn-r_critical(lambda)) ∈ [0.3,0.7]`.
- Geometry-only scout: on a 21×21 grid, both order `n=0` (first equatorial crossing) and `n=1` (second equatorial crossing) have 441/441 emitting points. Approximate scouted ranges:
  - `n=0`: source radius 6.24–8.92M, source phase −0.638 to −0.075, delay 98.67–102.54M;
  - `n=1`: source radius 6.06–12.42M, source phase −3.989 to −3.348, delay 122.41–129.74M.
- Screen acquisition: 8×8 fixed chart pixels per order; 13 observer times uniformly spanning 0–24M; source-time coordinate `tau=t_observer-delay+100M`.
- Primary pixel integration: tensor Gauss order 8. Independent numerical refinement: order 12 on the same pixels. No interpolation between the two rules.
- Primary physical solver: turning-point-regularized separated Kerr quadratures. Independent check: compactified-radius/polar ODE at 32 random chart points per order, frozen before source generation.
- One noise density is fixed from the direct constant-source RMS at `SNR0=300` and shared across both orders. A secondary `SNR0=100` stress uses the same calibration law. Noise variance is `sigma_Ω² × full pixel area`.
- Order labels are idealized and supplied. No unresolved-image, visibility-domain, telescope, polarization, scattering, or full-lensing-band claim.

### Physical gates

- Every primary and refinement ray must return an emitting event in the declared source annulus `[6,13]M` and the prescribed crossing/branch.
- Independent ODE comparison limits: source radius `2×10⁻⁶M`, wrapped phase `2×10⁻⁷ rad`, delay `2×10⁻⁶M`, redshift `2×10⁻⁷`.
- For the declared source basis and all fixed test-source fields, the order-8 versus order-12 response difference must be ≤`5×10⁻⁴` relatively and ≤`0.1` in the shared whitened norm. Failure blocks a movie-recovery claim but is retained.

## 2. Fixed movie representation and source families

The reconstruction space is a finite movie basis over `r∈[6,13]M`, `phi∈[−π,π)`, `tau∈[−32,28]M`:

- 5 clamped cubic radial B-splines;
- real azimuthal Fourier factors `1, cos(m phi), sin(m phi)` for `m=1,2,3` (7 factors);
- 17 compact, piecewise-linear temporal B-splines;
- total dimension 595.

The source norm is the discrete approximation to `∫|j−1|² r dr dphi dtau` on a fixed 32×64×31 evaluation grid. The same grid is used for frame metrics. The basis, norm, and temporal anchor `tau=0` are fixed before observations.

### In-class validation and test movies

Structured coefficient movies are generated by projecting broad analytic scenes into the 595-dimensional basis, then using the projected field as truth:

1. one orbiting broad hotspot;
2. two orbiting broad hotspots;
3. flare birth–motion–decay with radial drift.

Validation: 12 histories per family, seed 60061. Test: 20 new histories per family, seed 60062. No test history is used to choose regularization.

### Off-basis stress movies

Two analytic families are evaluated without replacing them by their projection:

4. a narrow hotspot with azimuthal width below the retained `m≤3` bandwidth;
5. a shearing spiral/arc whose phase depends on radius.

Test: 20 histories per family, seed 60063. These are source-mismatch diagnostics, not part of the in-class success gate.

All sources have fixed baseline one and are rescaled if necessary to remain nonnegative on the evaluation grid. Each test history receives four paired Gaussian noise draws at each SNR, seed 60064. Direct-only and direct-plus-order-1 comparisons use identical direct data/noise.

## 3. Fixed inverse methods

Stage A compares classical finite-model estimators only; neural methods are gated on Stage-A feasibility.

- Ridge/Tikhonov in the 595-dimensional basis with a fixed quadratic penalty combining radial curvature, azimuthal harmonic weight, and temporal curvature.
- TSVD of the source-normalized whitened operator as a diagnostic baseline.
- Direct-only arm uses `n=0` rows. Labelled arm uses stacked `n=0,1` rows and one shared noise density.
- Ridge candidates: `10^k`, `k∈{−6,−5,…,3}` times the fixed penalty scale. Select one candidate separately for each arm using median validation old-movie frame error at SNR0=300; freeze before test evaluation.
- TSVD thresholds: relative singular cutoffs `{10⁻⁴,3×10⁻⁴,10⁻³,3×10⁻³,10⁻²}` selected by the same validation population and then frozen.
- No test-dependent threshold or source-family-specific tuning.

Stage B (not automatically promoted) may train an amortized neural operator only if the labelled Stage-A ridge has a strictly longer primary movie interval than direct-only and a positive improvement on at least two of three in-class families. Stage B must use the same physical rows, movies, noise draws, and primary endpoint.

## 4. Primary movie endpoint

Evaluation frames are `tau=0,−2,…,−28M`, proceeding backward from the anchor. For an active truth frame (contrast RMS ≥0.03), define:

- contrast relative error `e_L2 = ||j_hat−j||₂ / ||j−1||₂`;
- structural correlation `corr`, the cosine similarity of mean-subtracted contrast images over the fixed radial–azimuthal grid.

A frame passes when `e_L2≤0.35` and `corr≥0.75`. A history/draw passes an interval `[−T,0]` only when every active frame in it passes; inactive frames must have reconstructed contrast RMS ≤0.05.

The **primary reliable movie span** is the largest `T` for which at least 95% of the in-class test history/draw pairs pass. A 90%-reliable span is reported as a secondary pilot endpoint. Uncertainty uses a paired, history-cluster bootstrap with 2,000 resamples; all four noise draws of a selected history move together.

Secondary endpoints:

- per-frame median and 90th-percentile `e_L2` and correlation;
- active-frame flux error and circular/radial centroid error;
- fraction of histories with any nonzero old interval;
- direct-versus-labelled paired source-error reduction by family;
- strict old-only data-fit and numerical-integration checks;
- visualization of one predeclared median-seed test movie, truth versus direct-only versus labelled.

A positive developmental result requires all of:

1. physical gates pass;
2. labelled primary 95%-reliable span is nonzero;
3. labelled span exceeds direct-only by at least 6M;
4. at least two of three in-class families show positive median paired reduction in integrated old-frame error;
5. no claim of full-annulus or observational movie recovery is made.

Failure of the 95% endpoint does not authorize changing the thresholds; the 90% endpoint and diagnostics are retained.
