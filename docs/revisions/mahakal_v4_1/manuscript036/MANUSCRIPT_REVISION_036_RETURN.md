## 2. The draft

It keeps the original title, the author list and the three organizing
questions, and it is a paper rather than a chronology of numerical debugging:
the audit appears in section 8 as what the corrections changed, not as the
narrative.

Every headline claim carries one of three tags. **[S]** structural mathematics,
true under stated assumptions about a declared operator and class. **[D]** a
reproduced discrete benchmark, valid under ten explicit pins. **[P]** a physical
acquisition or continuum statement — and no claim in the draft is supported at
that level. Where the pinned manuscript made one, the draft marks it rather
than deleting it.

Eleven of the eighteen claims are allowed in a candidate abstract. Seven are
not: the withdrawn operator-validation heading, the corrected order-resolution
attribution, the legacy-measure R2 count, and four findings that are true but
too easily misread as physics.

## 3. The three dispositions that matter

**The section 5 heading "Validated Computational Operator" is withdrawn**, not
softened. Measured as whitened detector vectors on identical supported
geometry, the coarse and fine representations of the transferred field differ
by 9.594e-03, 4.274e-02 and 5.116e-01 relative at orders 0, 1 and 2, against a
5e-4 component budget, on all 40 transferred channels. Section 7 now says which
parts of the discrete pipeline *are* qualified — hull geometry at 9.592e-08,
leaf assembly to 6.9e-18, the comparator on its tested cohorts — and section
8.2 says the physical quadrature is not among them.

**The order-resolution attribution is corrected, not queued.** `UNRESOLVED_IMAGE`
is an index-sum construction. The draft keeps the comparison as a statement
about two constructions of our own operator, with an explicit scope box, and
section 9.3 states that restoring the physical attribution needs a new physical
control and a new result — which a quadrature pass under a different gap would
not supply. This is the distinction ruling 036 asked for and the 034 route
table got wrong.

**R2's two operational directions are pinned to the frozen legacy measure.**
The draft states the corrected-measure interval 1 ≤ N ≤ 2 beside the legacy
count, with the reason: the order-0 row ratios span [0.500081, 1.000162], so
the per-order totals do not bound the rows. A corrected-measure recomputation
is listed as a dependency, not performed.
