## 2. One remaining leaf-bound defect: the fields are not all nonnegative

leaf.py::assemble places unresolved contributions in

    lower = known
    upper = known + sum(overlap * abs(field)).

This is valid for uncertainty about the presence of a KNOWN NONNEGATIVE point
field. It is not a bound for the signed fields already present in the declared
suite. With overlap 0.25, field -2 and uncertain indicator chi in [0,1], the
contribution lies in [-0.5,0]. The current construction gives [0,0.5], excluding
the physically possible negative contribution.

For a fixed field value f and nonnegative overlap w, the contribution interval is

    [w min(0,f), w max(0,f)].

For a magnitude envelope |f|<=M and unknown presence, it is [-w M,+w M]. With
chi in [c_lo,c_hi] and f in [f_lo,f_hi], evaluate all four endpoint products and
use their minimum/maximum before multiplying by w. Sum interval contributions
with the known response. Retain correlations only when they are actually known;
a componentwise interval box is conservative but not an arbitrary statistical
confidence region.

The companion signed_bounds_and_checks_033.py implements this interval operation
and tests positive, negative, mixed-sign and missing-envelope cases. It propagates
SUPPLIED intervals only. A finite midpoint field is not an envelope for every
point in a cut cell, and a corrected interval formula does not establish continuous
domain accuracy. Missing finite envelopes remain a blocker. The accepted overlap
partition result is not invalidated by this uncertainty-accounting correction.

Integrate the sign-aware logic into a NEW versioned consumer, retaining the 032
file unchanged. No campaign rays are required to demonstrate or correct this bug.
Test the actual consumer with negative g^3*cos/sin or signed screen-field fixtures,
not only the nonnegative occupancy case. Also validate nonnegative overlap weights
and reject missing bounds rather than manufacturing zeros.

## 3. Read the feasibility evidence at its actual scope

The six transition counts sum to 22236. The stated new point cost is therefore

    22236*(4+16) = 444720.

This excludes the remaining full-transfer and independent-reference work; it is
not a complete lifetime resource forecast or a proof of numerical impossibility.
The reported 182.7x and 1.77888x ratios are arithmetic comparisons with the old
native-only spendable amount and the lifetime cap. Under the now-selected
convention B there is no funded full-response validation campaign to compare
against. Do not rerun this expensive layout merely because it was once prescribed.

The unsuccessful wedge ladder rules out that declared wedge family under its
coverage requirements. It does not show that every local numerical test is empty
or unaffordable. Different orders have different boundary locations. A local
boundary diagnostic may use an order-specific patch, provided every comparison
FOR THAT ORDER uses the same physical patch and no cross-order science claim is
made from those different patches. A region with no transitions can still be a
useful smooth-field control; it cannot count as a boundary-validation case. The
zero-transition rejection was sensible for this boundary-only plan, not a
universal rule that a smooth region measures nothing.

The reported 13.9%/11.3% order-2 coverage is an area of clipped cells whose archived
CENTER has no usable transfer tuple. It is not a demonstrated fraction of lost
physical light, nor a proof that the desired integral cannot meet an error budget.
Some of those cells may include certified absent regions, some missing evaluations,
and some emitting fragments with unusable center data. Retain it as a coverage
warning and inventory its causes, not as a converged continuum missing-emission
measure. Do not erase uncertain contributions; where no response envelope exists,
the complete result remains unqualified.

The cache finding is accepted for its stated search: no recoverable 031 subpoint
values were found. Missing values cannot be reconstructed from their coordinates.
Avoid repeating that failure: every future completed chunk must commit a recoverable
payload and input identity before the summary. Cache identity must include screen
coordinate, order, geometry, observer convention, backend/policy and relevant
precision, not merely a parent-cell index or profile name.

## 4. The authorized comparator closeout

The 66 old unresolved records are development inputs, not a fresh holdout. Use
one versioned comparator incorporating the already identified indexed root
reduction, original polynomial checks, and fail-closed interval/convergence
checks. Preserve the previous source and its 66 outcomes. The root-separation
association is not yet a causal diagnosis; obtain intermediate evidence rather
than announce that deflation necessarily fixes every point.

Before evaluating new physical integrals, commit the code, root/branch policy,
precision and quadrature hierarchy, numerical error tolerances, failure stop rules,
cohort IDs, backend hashes and all query caps. Each primary/reference comparison
must retain integral values, endpoint limits, root residuals/conditioning, failure
reasons, estimated or rigorously bounded errors, and final decision margins.
Distinguish a quadrature estimate from a proof. Reject ambiguous/critical cases
rather than choose whichever answer matches the existing mask.

A. Diagnostic readback: all 66 unresolved records, with at most 384 charged units.
A native reevaluation is charged even on a known coordinate. A reference evaluation
is separately charged; repeated or alternate methods also count. Cached roots can
save the native call only if their values and origin are genuinely present. If the
same cause is demonstrated and the registered numerical conditions resolve these
cases, continue to B under the frozen protocol. Otherwise report exactly which
cases remain unresolved and stop; do not automatically launch a full integration.

B. Independent confirmation: at most 192 previously unused point/order IDs, including
near-turning events, healthy controls, physical absences and finite outside-annulus
cases, selected without target information. Freeze these IDs BEFORE this phase's
outcomes; no tuning on the confirmation panel. Use at most the remaining part of
the TOTAL 1024-unit cap. Reserve at least 576 of that total for this confirmation
and up to two independently charged reference evaluations per point where required.
The nominal 66+192=258 point count costs 516 with one native and one reference
per point, or 774 with two references. Thus '258 evaluations to clear' is a point
budget, not the correctly metered cost or a promised pass. Additional retries must
fit the committed cap and stopping rule; no silent refreeze resets counters.

If confirmation is unresolved, return a valid blocked result with all evidence.
If it passes, promote only COMPARATOR_NUMERICALLY_VALIDATED_ON_TESTED_COHORTS.
Neither result certifies the full field or any unsampled boundary. A confirmation
pass does not authorize physical integration under this ruling.

A 'small residual' cannot alone bound root-location error near a multiple root.
Preserve the multiplicity/cluster guard. The analytical reduction by root index
is a correct source-level change for simple roots, not justification to bypass
an ambiguity or repair a physically absent event.

## 5. Replacement integration design: one fixed quantity, adaptive accuracy

Authorize zero-new-physical-query design work for a single common-detector adaptive
integrator. This is a prospective alternative to the 18-grid layout, not an already
validated algorithm. Keep D026, all three orders, the eight observer times, sigma,
absolute clock, source annulus and existing physical target/nuisance model unchanged.

Core and fine maps may seed interpolation and independently check a fixed subset;
they need not each be exhaustively re-traced at every new subcell depth. Deduplicate
truly identical physical evaluations across representations. Reuse a valid transfer
tuple for multiple time samples and source test functions algebraically, without
claiming free new rays or recomputing the same tuple eight times. Do not pretend
missing archived tuples are available merely because a seed mesh exists.

The plan must cover the full chosen physical domain, not only a convenient wedge.
Track separately: hull-geometry error (already qualified), emitting-boundary error,
field quadrature/interpolation error, numerical reference error, and unknown-support
response. Use only geometry and declared-field error indicators for refinement.
Boundary roots may be localized as one-dimensional events on verified branches;
smooth interiors need not be refined at the same density. Multiple crossings,
tangencies and disconnected support must remain detectable, not assumed absent.
Full Cartesian materialization is not required for sparse cells/fragments.

For validated local response envelopes e_l, the triangle inequality supplies

    ||W (y-y_hat)|| <= sum_l e_l

on their stated domain. Without validated envelopes, use clearly labeled numerical
refinement evidence and independent checks, not a theorem. Require per-order and
per-field/time controls so cancellation or strong order-0 signal cannot hide error
in order 2. Preserve signed fields correctly. Keep the same response/mask/area
thresholds; adapt the numerical work, not the detector or acceptance threshold.
Three refinement checkpoints and an independently initialized or held-out check
are still needed for the proposed numerical qualification, but they need not be
18 independent copies of the legacy grid experiment.

A vector adaptive quadrature design with a single error-controlled quantity is
standard numerical practice; SciPy's official quad_vec documentation describes
adaptive subdivision by error and exposes intervals, errors and evaluation counts.
That is methodological context, not an instruction to apply a one-dimensional
library routine blindly to this two-dimensional discontinuous domain, nor a claim
that it meets our budget. No package upgrade or new physics backend is authorized.

Deliver one costed adaptive design from available geometry/payload metadata, with
its selection rule, cache keys, coverage accounting, expected and worst-case calls,
validation costs and stopping rule. If the possible costs cannot be established
without a pilot, say so and provide an explicitly bounded pilot proposal. There is
no requirement that the physical integral fit the old arbitrary 250000 lifetime
cap; there IS a requirement to request any changed authorization before executing.
No increase to that cap or a new integration phase is granted here.

## 7. Publication consequence

The present blocker is a failed/infeasible numerical validation design, not a
falsification of delayed-image history sensitivity. Conversely, 658 passing tests
and a better plan are not evidence that the physical common-sky operator is
accurate. The attached manuscript still claims a validated operator in section 5
and order-resolution attribution in sections 6.4 and 10.1; those statements require
the previously specified correction and must not be restored from this planning
return. Its organizing distinction between historical reach, dimension and
recovery should remain, with each number tied to its actual operator and norm.

A narrowly scoped ideal-order information paper does not logically require a
completed realistic acquisition campaign. Any such submission must, however,
revalidate or remove affected numerical claims and clearly exclude unvalidated
common-sky/physical attribution. This ruling neither rewrites nor freezes that
manuscript, and it does not claim the attached version is ready to submit.
