## 3. Restore the mathematical result, not the old slogan

036's abstract, contributions and conclusion again assert as [S] that source
'enrichment ... destroys identifiability' and compactness 'makes the direct image
exactly blind'. Neither is true without conditions. Compact temporal support can
lie inside the direct footprint; an injective operator can remain injective on a
larger finite source space. The relevant statement is support DISJOINTNESS, with
the nonvanishing converse and distinction between nonzero and independent columns.
This was already explicit in the supplied theorem and ruling 025, not a new hurdle.

Restore the actual forward operator, basis/synthesis map, source norm, noise/SNR
convention, localized probe, three interval definitions, nuisance profiling,
rank thresholds and estimator/endpoint definitions. The present document has
roughly one displayed acquisition formula block, no formal support theorem or
proof, no complete inverse specification, and no reference list or in-text source
citations. Tags cannot replace those components of a technical paper.

[S] may include a conditional theorem about a continuum mathematical model. It
must not be defined to exclude all continuum statements. [P] concerns validated
physical acquisition or quantitative approximation claims, not whether a theorem
mentions a continuum. Likewise [D] does not mean every change of coordinates
requires a rerun: proved invariant reparameterizations are distinct from changing
the experiment. Keep the tags in the audit matrix; ordinary precise scope language
can carry their meaning in the paper without a self-referential warning at every
paragraph. Replacement statements are supplied in REPLACEMENT_PASSAGES_037.md.

## 5. Reach: 144 M is a supremum, not an anchored span or recovered history

Section 4.1 repeats the old 60/84/144-M table as 'recoverable depth' and concludes
spin does not set reach. The E3C report explicitly calls that table the oldest
detectable probe age, a supremum on a 4-M grid. At 75 degrees, its resolved
anchor-connected spans are 112 M for spin zero and 116 M for the other three
spins; the direct spans are 108 and 112 M. The actual anchors are 32 and 28 M.
The original supplied PDF's page-20 table records these spans as well.

Preserve all three observables: oldest passing center, longest run anywhere, and
span reaching that geometry's anchor. A flat sampled supremum across four spins
does not establish physical spin independence. 'Recoverable' must be reserved for
the declared estimator endpoint, not a scalar detectability probe. The 12-geometry
positive extension survives at its discrete scope; these corrected labels do not
change its masks or numerical values.

Section 4.2 also restores the unqualified physical delay/spatial attribution from
0.98 versus 0.57. Amendment 023 identified both substitutions as index-paired
counterfactuals. Retain them as those synthetic comparisons, not causal evidence
that physical delay rather than spatial remapping supplies the gain. The later
index-sum scope box does not automatically qualify a different subsection.

## 6. Recovery claims need their regimes, estimators and metrics back

The original R1 report supports 48 -> 80 M in IN_CLASS_ID, IN_CLASS_OOD and the
mild OFF_GRID_OOD diagnostic, but 0 -> 0 in severe OFF_GRID_ID. '640 truths' is
the whole sealed bank, not a claim that every regime has the same stable span.
The four fitting-family successes are a separate breakdown. Restore the regime
table, epsilon=0.25, q=0.95, 4-M grid and truth-cluster uncertainty method. The
reported commitment preceded scoring; 'before an operator existed for them' is
an unnecessary stronger statement not established by that fact.

The 10x-SNR sentence is ambiguous between two distinct archived studies. R1's
structure-only stable span first appears at SNR_0=30000 (40 M direct, 76 M
resolved). The R1L stage-2R validation at 1000 has an aggregate materiality result,
not a sealed main and not stable structural-history recovery; its stable-span gain
remains zero. Neither can replace the other. The evidence ledger records both.

036 calls the level result 'coefficient-space ... not a source-function metric',
while the R1 report calls it a registered baseline-inclusive field metric. Do not
settle this by an adjective: supply its actual evaluation map/norm, source grid
and normalization from the runner/config. Coefficients used by an estimator do
not determine the meaning of its loss.

Retain the morphology estimates with estimator names: ridge 0.164 (lower bound
0.116), TSVD 0.133 (0.101), 60 histories and four paired draws, together with the
registered 0.10/0.05 floors and all four qualifications. Replace the repeated
'unresolved second image ... resolving the orders rather than photons' sentence,
even with its parenthetical disclaimer, by the supported comparison: labeled-
stack estimates improve under the declared model; the tested total-flux readout
does not reproduce the material gain; the index-sum control supplies no conclusion
about a physical unresolved spatial image. Add the uncertainty-withdrawal and
severe-mismatch negatives in actual numerical context, not just internal tokens.

## 7. Qualification and remaining gaps: do not overcorrect or overstate

9.592e-8 is the order-2 late tessellation comparison against its 1e-6 budget,
not the definition of complete hull accuracy against 2.5e-4. Hull qualification
remains accepted on its full conjunction of tests; just name the right metric.
The signed four-corner interval enclosure is valid for supplied intervals; it is
not the ONLY valid way to construct a conservative bound.

The three detector discrepancies compare two finite representations on matched
known support. They do not measure an exact distance from the continuum solution.
C13's sidecar/constructor repair did not reweight every archived R1/E3C/R2/HMT2
result. Do not say that the corrected measure was globally adopted while retaining
old numbers as though generated under it. Keep G10q's earlier noise-model change,
C02's reference-count normalization, and C13's geometry/weight inconsistency
separate, with downstream scope documented.

Ruling 036 already clarified that group-remainder radii are specific to one
possible error certificate, not a universal sixth physical law of validation.
Remove the restored demand that every qualifying method must use them. The two
listed physical workstreams are important, but 'exactly two experiments' is not
an exhaustive certification of all retained claims. The number of necessary
experiments depends on the chosen scientific claims and their operator/metric
lineage, which this matrix has not yet finished. Do not reopen the accepted
comparator, hull or sampled-absence closeouts to repair drafting mistakes.
