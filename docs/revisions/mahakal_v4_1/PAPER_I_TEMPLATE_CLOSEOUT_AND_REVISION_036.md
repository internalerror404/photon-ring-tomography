## 2. Accept the bound decomposition, with precise summary labels

The source summary fields are max_A_over_E, max_T_over_A and max_T_over_E, taken
separately over channels. Label the table columns accordingly. For a single channel
with nonzero denominators, (A/E)*(T/A)=T/E. In general the product of the separate
maxima is not the maximum of that product. U/max(E) is another aggregation and
cannot be multiplied into that channelwise identity.

Cancellation is NOT absent: max(A/E) is about 4.57 and 4.71 for the baseline at
orders 1 and 2. What is withdrawn is the attribution of the ENTIRE old 10-57x gap
to sign cancellation. The reported maximum detector-aggregation factors are larger
than the corresponding maximum cancellation factors in each order. That comparison
of maxima does not by itself prove T/A exceeds A/E at every individual channel.
Stronger channel-by-channel claims require the already exported channel ledger,
not multiplication or comparison of differently indexed maxima.

The exact E <= A <= T <= U hierarchy supplies a tighter deterministic diagnostic
without changing the estimator or field. This is now established enough for its
stated purpose. Do not start a further physical campaign just to make a bound look
smaller. Unknown fine-reference error and omitted support remain distinct unknowns.
