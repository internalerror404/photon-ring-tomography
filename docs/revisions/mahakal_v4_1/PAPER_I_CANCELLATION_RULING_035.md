## 2. Separate the three losses in the current bound

For one order and channel c, on identical known support, define

    K = W O, delta_c = F_hat,c - F_fine,c, v_pc = K[:,p] delta_pc,
    E_c = ||K delta_c||_2,
    A_c = ||K |delta_c|||_2,
    T_c = sum_p ||K[:,p]||_2 |delta_pc|,
    U   = sum_p ||K[:,p]||_2 max_c |delta_pc|.

O is nonnegative area overlap and W is positive diagonal whitening for the
fixed single-sky detector convention. Therefore, exactly for these arrays,

    E_c <= A_c <= T_c <= U.

E is the actual signed detector residual. A removes within-detector sign
cancellation while retaining Euclidean aggregation across detector pixels.
T additionally loses that detector-vector structure. U additionally chooses a
different worst channel at every source node. The 034 scenario uses U and divides
by max_c E_c. Its gap combines all three effects; it is not a measured pure
cancellation factor. With 324 positive contributions in 324 distinct detector
pixels, T/E=18 while A/E=1 and no sign cancellation exists. This is an executed
synthetic example, not a claim about the Kerr fields.

Compute the full E/A/T/U chain and per-channel denominators before inventing a
correlation or cancellation model. The deterministic bound A is already no larger
than T and requires no extra physical samples. Report undefined ratios where E=0
rather than forcing finite cancellation factors. Keep these as cached-discrepancy
bounds, not continuum-error certificates. Different noise covariance would require
its own derivation; do not silently apply this nonnegative-diagonal argument there.

p3_scenarios_034 also uses max_c ||y_ref,c|| as a shared denominator, but the
registered test is per channel. A channel with small reference norm can fail while
this shared-scale test passes. Evaluate E_c and any bound against that SAME channel's
reference norm, preserving the existing explicit zero-response convention.

## 3. The sorted tail is an oracle-edit scenario, not a refinement theorem

Removing the largest cached |delta| terms from the tail assumes those selected
errors become exactly zero, all unselected errors stay fixed, support and weights
stay fixed, and the fine sampled field is the target. Four child evaluations do
not establish any of those assumptions. Consequently the 63-94% counts and
308656 total are useful under an explicitly named ORACLE_ZERO_HEAD_CACHED_DEFECT
scenario, not a sufficient sample count for physical quadrature. The concentration
percentages describe the old aggregate U, not automatically the best allocation
for a signed detector norm.

Even perfect correction of a selected term need not monotonically reduce the
signed error: contributions 1 and -0.99 sum to 0.01; eliminating the first leaves
-0.99. A greedy rule must account for this possibility rather than assume every
locally improved sample reduces the current global residual.

To earn cancellation in a FUTURE physical error estimate, one possible sufficient
structure is a signed predicted contribution for each predefined geometric group,
plus validated remainder radii. For a coarse-versus-fine estimate this gives

    ||y_hat-y_true|| <= ||sum_g rhat_g|| + sum_g eta_g
                       + eta_fine_reference + eta_omitted_support.

This is conditional mathematics, not a measured bound for these maps. It requires
a valid estimate of the fine representation's own error and the omitted support,
neither supplied by the current comparison. Independent group uncertainty may be
replaced by a sharper validated joint bound, but not assumed correlation learned
and evaluated on the same residuals. Shared errors in coarse and fine data can
cancel in their difference and remain large relative to truth. A prior or learned
covariance is a model assumption, not a likelihood/numerical certificate.

## 4. A concrete zero-query representation test

The inherited forty transferred columns at eight observer times have an exact
five-real-template structure. Define

    H_0(xi) = g(xi)^3,
    H_T(xi) = g(xi)^3 exp[-i (2*pi/T) Delta(xi)], T in {20,40}.

At observer time t, the two harmonic fields are
Re[exp(i*2*pi*t/T) H_T] and Im[exp(i*2*pi*t/T) H_T]. Thus H_0 plus real/imaginary
parts of H_20 and H_40 reconstruct all forty transferred diagnostic columns.
The fixed detector integration commutes with that real-linear reconstruction.
The six screen channels remain separate. This changes algebraic storage/work,
not the physical source, spectrum, observing schedule, or number of independent
observations. It is NOT an eightfold ray speedup and does not describe arbitrary
source movies or the full L224 source operator.

The current path bilinearly interpolates g and coordinate time and THEN forms
cubes and trigonometric phases. Compare it with ONE additional candidate:
form the five templates at valid core nodes FIRST, then bilinearly interpolate
those templates and apply the same detector. Nonlinear transformation and
interpolation do not commute. Neither ordering is uniformly more accurate; a
phase undersampled by a whole period can defeat template interpolation. The
purpose is a bounded comparison, not a promised optimization.

Use exactly the same finite-stencil support for the primary paired comparison.
Report an all-corners-emitting restriction as a separate coverage sensitivity,
not as a way to drop hard points until a metric passes. Preserve phase, time-origin
and redshift-sign conventions and classify nonfinite tuples before transforming;
no silent NaN-to-zero or negative-redshift repair is introduced. Derive every old
channel after template reconstruction and verify the identity on synthetic and
cached finite arrays. The accompanying local check finds maximum point identity
error 1.64e-14 and detector identity error 2.66e-15 on synthetic data. No campaign
improvement is measured by this review.

Evaluate both candidates once under a committed archive-audit specification;
no interpolation-family sweep, learned fit or target-informed tuning. Previously
inspected fine tuples are a development comparison, not fresh holdout truth.
Report positive, null or worse outcomes without changing the criterion.

## 5. Interpretation and export corrections that do not reopen physical campaigns

The 'same radial leg' condition in p1_stencil_034 is radius span <= 0.25 times
mean radius, not a branch test. Zero order-2 survivors means zero under that
small-span heuristic. It does not prove that every cell mixes physical legs or
that no smooth representation exists. A linear function can have a large span
and still interpolate exactly. Use SMALL_RADIUS_SPAN_HEURISTIC and retain
BRANCH_UNVERIFIED absent actual branch metadata. The observed large spans and
large bilinear response differences remain useful evidence about this particular
representation; do not call a threshold on variation a physical impossibility.
The raster check samples the first 200 lookups, so 'confirmed per node' overstates
that check. A full cached coordinate-index verification costs no physical query.

The six screen residuals are zero because both branches receive the same screen
fields and same overlap operator. That is an implementation-consistency control,
not independent absolute validation of the shared geometry. A shared wrong
operator would also yield zero. The previously accepted geometry has its own tests.

The C2 writer pops the vectors dictionary and stores only its reference member.
It never writes the estimated vectors or delta, despite the following comment.
Read the actual NPZ keys before relying on a complete pair. Reproduce the missing
cached arrays in a NEW output from authenticated inputs, without new physical
queries and without overwriting 034. Store reference, both estimates, signed
residuals, template arrays, per-channel norms, support IDs, and CSR overlap arrays
with dimensions, units and hashes. Verify readback before rendering the summary.

The incomplete covered domain is an independent blocker. Order 2's compared area
is 1.0293693 of the fine-center-labeled emitting area 1.6385979 M^2, or 62.82%.
That area partition is a declared discretized diagnostic, not a continuum-domain
certificate. No cancellation model on the covered subset bounds the missing
0.6092286 M^2 without a physical field envelope. Preserve the missing contribution
as unknown, never assume favorable cancellation with it. The two-profile finite
comparison does not establish that the fine representation is converged either.
