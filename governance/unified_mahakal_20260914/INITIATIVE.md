## Central scientific question

Under what geometry, noise, acquisition and source-model conditions does higher-order light permit reliable recovery of source history that direct observations cannot recover, while unknown emission is inferred jointly?

The same forward map, historical target, source class, nuisance assumptions, observation convention and scoring domain must connect physical reach, identifiability and reconstruction. A rank result in one representation is not proof of a movie in another. Retain multiple hypotheses and joint uncertainty; count the direct likelihood once; no final single plug-in background subtraction.
